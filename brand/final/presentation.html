What we heard

The brief

An editorial e-magazine featuring Ata Manobo, Bagobo-Tagabawa, Mandaya, Mansaka and Obo Manuvu. The identity should make the five distinct homelands visible without suggesting a single shared culture or borrowing community-specific symbols.

editorialdistinctgroundedwelcoming

A distinctive, simple mark that stays readable at small sizes, works in one colour, and respects the distinct identities of the five featured communities.

Awit at Alamat — final identity2
Final design

Five Homelands

Five independent contour strokes evoke the five featured homelands.

  • Uses the exact smooth-wave direction selected by the user.
  • The separate strokes avoid merging the communities into one cultural symbol.
  • Forest Night and Turmeric carry the site's existing visual identity into the logo.
