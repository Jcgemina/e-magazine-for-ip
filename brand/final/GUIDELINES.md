# Awit at Alamat — Logo Guide

## The mark

Five independent, flowing contour strokes stand for the five homelands represented in this magazine. They are an editorial metaphor, not a map or a claim that the featured communities share one culture. The mark uses no community-specific or sacred motifs.

## Files

- `awit-at-alamat-horizontal.svg` — primary lockup for light backgrounds.
- `awit-at-alamat-horizontal-dark.svg` — turmeric-and-ivory lockup for dark backgrounds.
- `awit-at-alamat-stacked.svg` — square and centered compositions.
- `awit-at-alamat-symbol.svg` — primary symbol.
- `awit-at-alamat-symbol-small.svg` — optimized favicon and small-symbol artwork.
- `awit-at-alamat-wordmark.svg` — outlined wordmark by itself.
- `variants/` — black, white, and turmeric lockups, with large transparent PNGs.
- `../../assets/brand/` — favicon, app/PWA icons, and the website's icon files.

## Clear space and minimum size

Keep at least one contour stroke-width of clear space around the mark. Use the small symbol for favicons; the primary symbol should not be used below 24 px. Keep horizontal lockups at least 128 px wide and stacked lockups at least 128 px wide so the wordmark remains readable.

## Colour

The logo extends the site's existing palette; the colours do not identify individual communities.

| Colour | HEX | RGB | Approx. CMYK |
|---|---|---|---|
| Forest Night | `#15221E` | 21, 34, 30 | 38, 0, 11, 87 |
| Turmeric | `#E6C547` | 230, 197, 71 | 0, 14, 69, 10 |
| Warm Stone | `#FAFCF8` | 250, 252, 248 | 1, 0, 2, 1 |

Use the Forest Night mark on light backgrounds. On dark Forest Night backgrounds, use the gold-and-ivory horizontal lockup or a one-colour white variant. CMYK values are approximate conversions; no Pantone match is specified. Proof printed colours with a printer before production.

## Lettering and artwork

The wordmark is outlined from Big Shoulders Display ExtraBold, matching the site's display type. The font is licensed under the SIL Open Font License; no font file is bundled with the logo. The contour artwork is intentionally stroke-based, with rounded ends and joins.

## Avoid

Do not stretch, rotate, add effects, recolour individual contours, rearrange the lockup, or place it over a busy image without a calm field. Do not add community-specific patterns or symbols without permission from the relevant community. Do not present the mark as a shared cultural emblem or as evidence of community endorsement.

## Open item

No trademark clearance or community approval has been performed. Check relevant trademark registers and seek feedback from the represented communities before treating the logo as an official community-endorsed identity.
